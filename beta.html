Why We Exist

A different kind of creator platform.

Most platforms built for women creators pull in one of two directions. One says: strip down, sell your body, chase attention. The other says: become a corporation, hustle harder, learn seventeen tools. Neither is a home.

Luma House is being built for a third kind of creator. The mother whose meal plans keep three families sane. The organizer whose closet system her friends beg to copy. The daughter caring for her father who has learned things every family will one day need. The teacher who wrote a spelling curriculum on nights and weekends. These women have real value. What they do not have is a place designed for them.

We are building that place. It is quiet, dignified, warm. It uses AI to remove the parts of the work no one enjoys — the formatting, the funnels, the copy — so the creator can focus on the part only she can do. It treats her audience with respect. It pays her fairly.

Come build it with us. — The Luma House team

What we believe

  • Real knowledge is worth as much as credentials, and often more.
  • A woman should never have to trade her dignity for income.
  • Community beats followers. A hundred readers who trust you is a business.
  • AI is a studio assistant, not a ghostwriter. Your voice stays yours.

  • Slow, thoughtful growth outlasts hockey-stick vanity.

Founding Terms

What a founding seat actually includes.

Being early with us should mean something. Not vague promises — real terms, in writing.

01

Zero platform fees for your first twelve months.

No subscription. No take rate. You keep one hundred percent of what your community pays you. After year one, founding creators are grandfathered into permanent reduced pricing.

02

Your storefront, professionally set up by us.

You send us your notes, your voice memos, your rough drafts. We turn them into a polished storefront, three formatted digital products, and a first email sequence. In days, not months.

03

Weekly one-on-one with the founder.

Thirty minutes a week for the first ninety days, directly with the person building this. Not customer support. Not a form. A phone call. Your feedback shapes the product.

04

A permanent Founding Creator badge.

A discreet mark on your profile that tells buyers you were here first. It becomes more valuable the bigger Luma House becomes.

05

Featured placement at public launch.

When we open Luma House to the world, your storefront is on the home page, in our press outreach, and in every launch email. This is exposure we cannot recreate for later joiners.

06

Legal and tax templates written for you.

Model terms of service for your storefront, plain-English disclaimers for wellness and financial content, and 1099-K guidance from actual professionals. You focus on your work.

Common Questions

The honest answers.

If you have a question we haven't answered, put it in your application. We read every one.

Is Luma House built yet?
A working prototype exists — you may have seen it. The full production platform is being built now, alongside cohort one. Founding creators are essentially co-designing what ships. That is exactly why founding terms are what they are.
What size audience do I need?
There is no minimum. We would rather work with a thoughtful creator whose 400 email subscribers trust her deeply than someone with 40,000 passive followers. Trust travels further than reach.
Will I own my audience and my content?
Completely. You can export your subscriber list, your content, and your payment history at any time. Your customers are your customers. This is a founding principle and it will be in the terms of service in writing.
What does the AI actually do?
It formats. It structures. It drafts an email sequence from a voice memo. It turns a Google Doc into a designed PDF. It does not write in your voice, publish for you, or invent claims you did not make. The moment it feels like a ghost, we have failed.
Who is behind Luma House?
A small team led by a Houston-based founder with a decade of product and design work behind him, quietly assembling an advisory circle of women creators, educators, and operators. The full team page comes at public launch. Founding creators meet everyone by name.
Where is the catch?
The catch is that we are in beta. Things will break. Features you want will not exist yet. You are being invited because your feedback will bend the product, and that is a genuine cost of your time. In return you get everything on this page. If that trade sounds fair, keep going.
